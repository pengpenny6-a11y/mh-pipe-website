2. Generated product and application assets
Product assets: /assets/products/tee.png, coupling.png, elbow.png, pipe.png.
Scene assets: /assets/scenes/home.png, gas.png, cooling.png, industry.png, fire.png.
These are newly generated concept visuals matching the selected design direction, not photographs of verified SKUs, installations or MH PIPE facilities.
Each application now uses its own generated spatial illustration. home.png represents building-water context; gas.png represents building-gas context; fire.png represents fire-protection context. None is an installation drawing or an engineering system design.
Final geometry, socket profiles, matching dimensions, materials and application suitability require verified product data. No pressing deformation, tool compatibility, system performance or engineering validation is claimed.
The scene assets are 1536 × 1024. Hotspot coordinates in src/content.js were visually aligned to these final images on 2026-11-06, using percentages of the complete image. They identify visible generic component forms, not verified product compatibility or installation compliance.
Gas and fire copy explicitly describes a spatial concept. These are application-selection illustrations only; none may be described as a verified gas, plumbing or fire-protection installation.
No home.png hotspot is assigned to an elbow: there is no clear elbow fitting in that image. Wall clamps and pipe supports are not treated as saleable coupling fittings.
gas.png was generated as an original 1536 × 1024 RGB image on 2026-11-06 and used without image editing. Its two selected hotspots are the upper branch tee (66.1%, 44.3%) and the kitchen branch elbow (46.5%, 73.0%). No gas-image valve or wall clamp is labelled as a coupling.
fire.png was generated as an original 1536 × 1024 image on 2026-11-06. Its two selected hotspots are the branch tee (68.1%, 35.1%) and the right-hand elbow (86.6%, 34.4%).
